# Data Visualization

> Data visualization practice focused on turning datasets into understandable insights.

## 🎯 Product Management Perspective

Analytics becomes useful when it helps someone make a decision.

This repository demonstrates foundational work relevant to AI/Data Product Management:

**Data → KPI → Visualization → Insight → Decision**

## 📊 Product Analytics Questions

When turning analysis into a product decision, consider:

- What user or business question are we answering?
- Which KPI represents success?
- What segment or cohort matters?
- What changed over time?
- What action should the insight enable?
- How should the result be communicated?

## 🔍 Recommended Case-Study Structure

For future analytics projects, document:

### 1. Problem
What decision needs better data?

### 2. Dataset
Where did the data come from and what are its limitations?

### 3. Metrics
Which KPIs are being measured and why?

### 4. Analysis
What patterns or relationships appear?

### 5. Product Insight
What could a product team do differently because of the analysis?

### 6. Experiment
How would you test whether the proposed change improves the KPI?
